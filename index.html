Wi‑Fi Troubleshooting Guide

Use this page to quickly check the most common Wi‑Fi problems and fixes.

1. Check the basics

  • Confirm the Wi‑Fi is enabled on your device.
  • Make sure you are connecting to the correct network name (SSID).
  • Verify the password is correct and that the signal strength is not too weak.

2. Restart the connection

  • Turn Wi‑Fi off and on again on your device.
  • Restart the modem/router if the internet is unstable.
  • Try moving closer to the router to rule out weak signal strength.

3. Forget and reconnect

  • Open your Wi‑Fi settings.
  • Select the network and choose Forget.
  • Reconnect using the correct credentials.

4. Check for device or driver issues

  • Restart your laptop or phone.
  • Update the Wi‑Fi adapter or network driver.
  • Check whether another device on the network is working normally.

5. When to contact support

  • If the router is not broadcasting a network.
  • If several devices fail at the same time.
  • If the internet fails even when other networks work.
